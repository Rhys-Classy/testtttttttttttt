SYNDICATE.DROP — Pokemon TCG drop monitor
==========================================

Double-click SyndicateDrop.exe. That's it — for a first install AND for every update.

- Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info" then "Run anyway".
- It finds your existing watchlist, orders and Telegram bot, stops any old copy,
  installs itself, sets itself to start with Windows, and restarts.
- Your Telegram bot then says "installed/updated and running".

First install only: the dashboard opens; press CONNECT TELEGRAM and follow the 3 steps.

After that, run everything from Telegram (send /help to your bot).
Keep the PC on and awake (Settings > System > Power > sleep when plugged in: Never).
