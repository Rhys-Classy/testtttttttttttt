# Wholesale Pokémon TCG in Australia: research (11 Nov 2026)

## How stock reaches Australia

```
The Pokémon Company International
        │  licenses one distributor per country
        ▼
Croftminster Pty Ltd (trades as Banter Toys & Collectibles): exclusive AU/NZ distributor
        │  trade accounts, allocated
        ▼
Big W · EB · Target · Kmart · JB · hobby stores
```

Nintendo Australia handles some Pokémon licensing and marketing here, because The Pokémon Company
has no Australian branch. The Pokémon Center online store now ships to Australia (from the US,
2+ weeks, no free shipping).

## 1. Banter Toys (Croftminster): the official one

- Croftminster's LinkedIn says it is the exclusive Pokémon TCG distributor for Australia and New Zealand. The International Licensing Organisation lists Banter Toys as the division Pokémon International appointed.
- bantertoys.com.au has an **Apply For Trade Account** link. It blocked automated reading, so the exact criteria are unconfirmed.
- What distributors usually want (from an Australian forum report and US sources, so indicative only):
  - An **ABN** and a registered business name
  - A **working online store with checkout** (Shopify is fine), or a physical shop
  - Proof you already sell: eBay, Whatnot or Facebook Marketplace history helps
- Expect: hot sets are allocated by account history. New accounts get evergreen product (tins, ETBs from older sets) first, and earn allocation by buying steadily.
- Classy Clothing Company already has an ABN and a store build in progress. A "Collectibles" collection on that Shopify, or a separate small store, satisfies the "working online store" test fastest.

## 2. Japanese sealed (volume)

- **Fudakura Australia** (fudakura-australia.com) lists AUD case pricing ex-GST, e.g. Storm Emeralda booster box A$281.20 at 6, ETBs about A$50–58. Free shipping over A$3,000. No approval needed.
- ⚠ **Red flags:** Bitcoin is the only on-page payment method; other methods come "by email within 12 hours". No ABN shown. Treat as **unverified**. If you test it: one small order, a card that allows chargebacks, never crypto.
- Local Japanese retail for margin math: Troll Australia about A$130–180 per box; GD Games A$165–240.
- Import costs: orders over A$1,000 need an import declaration. Expect **10% GST**. Duty on Japan-origin goods is likely nil under the Japan–Australia trade agreement, but confirm with a customs broker before the first big order.
- Safer alternative: buy direct from established Japanese exporters with PayPal and a long eBay or marketplace history.

## 3. Secondary "wholesale": skip

Grailborne (AU) says partner pricing lands "at or below" distributor minimums, but it sources at secondary-market prices and says plainly that it is not an authorised distributor. Carta Distribution (US$250 minimum, ships to AU) is the same model. Fine for filling a shelf, not for profit.

## Next actions

1. **Apply for the Banter Toys trade account** at bantertoys.com.au → Apply For Trade Account. Use the Classy Clothing ABN, or register a business name for the cards side.
2. Stand up a simple Shopify "collectibles" store (needed for #1 and for selling).
3. Run the drop monitor for retail releases in the meantime.
